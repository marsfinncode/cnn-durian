# Privacy Policy for YOEDO

**Effective Date:** 2 October 2026

Thank you for choosing to be part of our community at YOEDO ("we", "us", or "our"). We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy.

## 1. Information We Collect
The YOEDO application operates completely offline on your device. **We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data, images, or internet traffic.**

## 2. Camera Permissions
Our application requires access to your device's Camera to function properly. 
* **Why we need it:** The camera is used strictly in real-time to scan durian leaves for disease detection.
* **How data is processed:** All image processing and artificial intelligence (AI) inference (using TensorFlow Lite) is done locally on your smartphone's processor. 
* **No data is uploaded:** We do not upload your camera feed, images, or scanning results to any external servers, databases, or third parties.

## 3. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Thus, you are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.
